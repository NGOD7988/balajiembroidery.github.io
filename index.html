The Studio Story

Preserving Heritage Needles in the Heart of Haryana

Founded with a passion for preserving traditional Indian embroidery techniques while adapting them to modern silhouettes, Balaji Embroidery brings together multigenerational artisans specializing in Zardozi bullion, intricate Aari tambour work, and fine silk thread-painting.

Every stitch is placed by hand using 100% natural mulberry silk and mercerized cotton threads. When you send your cherished jacket, saree, or kurta to our studio, you become part of a living craft tradition that values slow fashion and heirloom longevity over mass manufacture.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing ready-made art and sending in your garments.

After configuring your order online, the website generates an official Intake Slip. Simply place your clean garment inside a secure courier polybag or box with the printed slip inside. If you selected Doorstep Reverse Pickup, our courier partner (Delhivery/Shiprocket) will collect it directly from your address. If you chose Self-Ship, dispatch it to our Gurugram, Haryana address and input your tracking code in your order portal.
Yes! We specialize in artistic visible mending. When configuring your custom order, upload a photo showing the damaged area and add a note. Our artisans will strategically place florals, motifs, or geometric borders directly over the imperfection to transform it into a one-of-a-kind art piece.
Ready-to-ship art and apparel are dispatched within 24–48 hours. Custom mail-in garments typically require 7 to 10 working days at our Haryana workshop once received, depending on stitch density and complexity. You can track progress in real time via our live tracking portal.
We recommend gentle hand-washing inside-out in cold water using mild silk/wool detergent, or professional dry cleaning for heavy Zardozi metallic work. Never wring or bleach. Always iron on the reverse side with a thin cotton cloth placed over the embroidery.
